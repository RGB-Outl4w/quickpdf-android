Find anything in your PDFs, in seconds.

QuickPDF is a small, fast PDF reader for Android, built for studying. Open a textbook, a scanned book or your own notes and search the whole thing instantly.

Made for long documents

Textbooks run to hundreds of pages. QuickPDF is built to get you to the right one quickly and stay out of the way while you read.

Instant full-text search

Every page is indexed in the background and cached. Matches are highlighted on the page, with a results list and previous/next navigation.

Scanned books, searchable

Pages without a text layer can be recognized on the device: ML Kit for Latin-script languages, Tesseract for Russian.

Fast navigation

Drag the edge scroller through hundreds of pages, or jump by table of contents, bookmarks, page number or thumbnails.

Comfortable reading

Continuous scrolling, pinch and double-tap zoom, a distraction-free full screen and a night mode for pages.

Password-protected PDFs

Encrypted documents open after you enter the password. Their text is never written to storage.

Private by design

No accounts, analytics or ads. Your documents never leave the device.

Search that forgives

Real documents are messy. QuickPDF finds what you meant, not only what you typed.
